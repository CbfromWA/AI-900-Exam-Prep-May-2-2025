Module 1: AI Workloads

Here are my notes on the basic principles of Artificial Intelligence.

Key Concept: Machine Learning is the foundation of AI. It allows computers to learn from data without being explicitly programmed.
  • Regression: Predicting numeric values (e.g., price of a house).
  • Classification: sorting items into categories (e.g., is this email spam?).
  • Clustering: Grouping similar items (e.g., customer segmentation).
